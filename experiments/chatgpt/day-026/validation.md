# Day026 validation

Validated 2026-09-30 before the creation commit.

## Owner-direction check

1. **Concrete curiosity and specimen:** when does a surrounding frame become the square's outline? Three simultaneous square fields make open, adjustable and coincident boundary states directly visible.
2. **Variable and constants:** the adjustable frame's equal gap changes from 18% to 0%, making its box 66–30%. Square size, color, orientation, exact center and field remain constant; the frame center, color and stroke treatment also stay fixed.
3. **Why visual and interactive:** prose cannot supply direct evidence that all centers remain aligned while four gaps close together. A live middle state and persistent endpoints make the geometry inspectable without relying only on memory.
4. **Day007–009 principle:** the specimen leads, its visual relation is the interface, and the meaningful change is reversible. The three-field proof follows today's symmetric-boundary question rather than copying an older palette, type composition or page shell.
5. **Challenge and evidence:** frame size, enclosed area, stroke painting, color contrast, labels, panel order and range motion history can explain any reported role change. Implemented states and model geometry were verified; browser captures and participant observations do not exist.

## Research-led form

The 2026-09-24 border-ownership study makes the ownership of an edge a current question but uses brief targets and masks, so this work does not imitate its apparatus or claim its result. Day025's translated frame left asymmetry and frame centrality as confounds; today's centered contraction removes those two while explicitly accepting frame size and enclosed area as remaining changes. Malevich's 1918 *White on White* provides a historical counterexample in which imprecise, low-contrast borders make space feel open; exact high-contrast boxes here test a different construction without reproducing the painting. CSS Box Model terminology supplies reproducible edges. Non-text Contrast and Dragging Movements guidance led to contrasting essential graphics, text equivalents, native keyboard input and direct-click presets.

Code-native geometry was selected because it preserves measurable responsive insets and reversible intermediate states. A still would remove interaction; video would add timing; photography or generated imagery would add texture, resampling and rights variables. This is a research-led medium decision, not a code-only rule.

Relevant completed review lesson: Day006 and Day008 showed that disclosure and side-by-side comparison do not neutralize perception. Persistent endpoints and optional construction may reduce memory dependence, but they can also prime the expected reading. No improvement claim is made.
